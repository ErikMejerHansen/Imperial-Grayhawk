Edran

Younger Brother of the Earl of Northkeep

← Back to Archive

Where You Met

  • His residence in Grayhawk City, where you delivered Winvarle's sealed correspondence

Appearance

  • In public: a doddering old uncle — stooped, soft of spine, watery-eyed, hands that fumble at the wine cup
  • In private, the years fall away: a vigorous middle-aged man, straight-backed, with the steady gaze of someone used to weighing men quickly and rarely being wrong
  • Plain, well-cut clothes of an older fashion — the sort that draw no second glance at funerals or weddings
  • Hands that are softer than a soldier's but quicker than they look

Relationships

  • Younger brother of Earl Winvarle of Northkeep — the two share a private cipher of long standing, learned in boyhood
  • Trusts Captain Sereth Halvar of the Order of Heironeous — "He can be trusted, which is a sentence I can compose about very few people at present"
  • Knows the gossip of the capital from the inside, having spent years being talked over rather than to

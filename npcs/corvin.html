Corvin Thale

Dawnblade of the Sunlit Throne

← Back to Archive

Where You Met

  • The coaching inn called the Seven Spokes, on the road south from Northkeep, where he interviewed you after the mutant ambush

Appearance

  • A man of middle years, thin and unhurried — the kind of thinness that comes from discipline rather than hardship
  • Pale, severe face; a mouth that does not smile to put others at ease
  • Eyes that do not blink as often as you expect, and rest on you for a beat too long after he has stopped speaking
  • Hands that move with the precision of a man who once set bones for a living: clean, deliberate, never wasted
  • Dark riding clothes worn beneath a plain dawn-marked surcoat; the sigil of the Sunlit Throne worked small at the throat, as if he does not need it to be larger

Relationships

  • Unknown

Notes

  • Rode north from the Seven Spokes with a hundred cloaks at his back, bound for Northkeep

Umbrusk's Tall Tale of Ponder, Dirt, and the Goblin Birthday Debacle

As told by Umbrusk the Barkeep at the Sign of the Slumbering Drake

◆

Umbrusk clears his throat, leans on the bar, and gives the room that look — the one that says "You're about to hear something you'll wish you hadn't, but you'll be glad you did."

"Right then. Gather close. This one's about your brave companions, and the day they nearly saved Northkeep."

He gestures with his tankard.

◆

Ponder's Stand at the Burning Church

"First off, Ponder. Gods bless the lad. Scrawny mage, robes always half-buttoned, hair like he's been struck by lightning twice. But brave? Oh, braver than sense allows."

He taps the bar for emphasis.

"Three ettercaps — not one, not two, three — skitter up the church walls like nightmares with too many eyes. And who's standing in the doorway? Ponder. Alone. With that prosthetic arm of his, a badger, and the kind of optimism only the doomed possess."

A few patrons chuckle.

"And Dirt — that's the badger, smartest creature in the whole affair. Soon as the fighting starts, he bolts off to alert the guards. Ponder claims it was a tactical maneuver. I say Dirt just knows when to leave a sinking ship."

He shrugs.

"Still, Ponder held them off long enough for the sick to escape. Roof came down just as the guards dragged him out. Brave fool nearly got himself baked like a festival pie."

◆

The Goblin Boss's Birthday Party

"Now, you'd think after nearly dying in a fire, they'd rest. Maybe have a drink. Maybe two. Maybe ten. But no — off they wander across the river to the Caves of Chaos. Because apparently the Caves weren't chaotic enough already."

He smirks.

"And what do they find? A goblin birthday party. Streamers. Cake. The works. The boss herself wearing a paper crown like she's queen of the underworld."

He raises an eyebrow.

"And what do our heroes do? Do they sing? Do they offer gifts? Do they show basic birthday etiquette?"

He pauses.

"No. They eat her cake. All of it. Didn't even pretend to savor it."

The tavern erupts in laughter.

"And the goblin boss — she's crying at this point, mind you — starts spilling her guts about the gift Tomas the messenger promised her. 'The city of Northkeep!' she says. 'All I have to do is light some campfires!'"

Umbrusk snorts.

"Campfires. That's the plan. That's the whole plan!"

◆

The Cube, the Patrol, and the Groaning Cave

"Anyway, after ruining the birthday of the century, the party leaves the goblins tied up and gagged — which, frankly, is the nicest thing they did all day — and runs straight into a gelatinous cube."

He makes a wobbling gesture with his hand.

"Big, clear, hungry thing. Like a murderous jelly dessert. They escape with only minor burns, which is impressive, considering cubes don't usually let you leave with your skin still attached."

He leans in.

"Then they find the missing patrol. Poor sods. Sick as the rest of Northkeep. Whatever's plaguing the city, it's over there too."

A hush settles.

"And that's where the tale ends. For now. Standing before the last cave. A low groaning coming from the dark. The kind of sound that makes your bones remember they're mortal."

He straightens, wiping the bar with a rag that's seen better centuries.

"Now drink up. If the gods are kind — and they rarely are — the next chapter won't be the last."
